# Northwind Logistics: Midwest Hub Expansion — Business Case

## Executive summary

Northwind Logistics proposes to open a cross-dock hub in Columbus, Ohio, to serve customers in Ohio, Indiana and Michigan. The hub replaces two leased depots whose leases end in 2027 and lets us consolidate less-than-truckload shipments that today travel through Chicago. We have looked carefully at a range of options over the past year and we believe this proposal represents the best balance between cost, service and risk for the company. The total capital cost is $4.2M. Shipment volume through the hub is expected to grow 18 % per year, and the investment pays back in 2.1 years.

## 1. Background

Northwind moves 2,400 less-than-truckload shipments a week in the Midwest. About 40 % of them pass through the Chicago terminal even when origin and destination are both east of Indianapolis, which adds a day to transit and $38 per shipment in linehaul cost.

## 2. Market

2.1 The Midwest less-than-truckload market was worth $9.8B (Freight Analytics, 2019). Our share is 1.6 %.

## 3. Demand forecast

3.2 We expect shipment volume through the hub to grow 18 % per year from 2027 to 2031, driven by e-commerce growth across the three states.
